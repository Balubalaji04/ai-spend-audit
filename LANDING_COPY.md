## FAQ

### Q1: Is this actually free? Will you charge me later?

Yes, it is free. SpendScope does not ask for a credit card, does not start a trial, and does not charge you later. Credex offers discounted AI credits, so the audit is free because it helps teams discover whether there is a real savings opportunity.

### Q2: Do I have to create an account or log in?

No. You enter your AI tools, plans, seats, and approximate monthly spend, then get results immediately. No account, no password, no workspace connection.

### Q3: How accurate are the savings numbers?

The audit uses public list prices and straightforward rules, so it is best treated as a savings estimate, not an invoice-grade accounting report. It is accurate enough to spot obvious plan mismatch, duplicate tools, oversized team plans, and high API spend worth reviewing.

### Q4: What does Credex do with my email if I share it?

Credex uses your email to send your audit results and, if your savings look meaningful, follow up about relevant AI credit opportunities. We do not sell your email list. You can ignore the follow-up or unsubscribe.

### Q5: What if my audit shows I'm already spending optimally?

That is a good result. You can use the report as confirmation that your current AI stack is lean, and you can still leave an email if you want updates when pricing changes or new discount opportunities appear.
